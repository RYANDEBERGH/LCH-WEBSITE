### What we collect

We only collect what we need for the purpose you came to us for.

- **Contact and partnership forms:** your name, email, phone number, company, the type of enquiry and anything you choose to tell us in your message.
- **Newsletter sign-up:** your name and email address, and a record of your consent.
- **Technical information:** like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and the date and time of a visit. We use this to keep the site secure and working properly.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16.

### How we use it

- To respond to your enquiry and follow up on a potential project, partnership or business relationship.
- To send you news about our projects, if you have opted in.
- To operate, secure and improve this website.
- To meet legal, regulatory and record-keeping obligations.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information.

### Consent

By submitting a form, you consent to us using your information for the purpose it was provided. You can withdraw consent at any time by unsubscribing or by contacting our Privacy Officer, subject to legal or contractual limits.

Some of these providers store data outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be accessible to authorities under local law.

We may also disclose information where required or permitted by law, or as part of a business transaction such as a merger or sale, subject to appropriate protections.

### Cookies and third-party content

This website does not use advertising cookies. Some pages include content from third parties, such as maps, LinkedIn and Instagram posts, and links to project websites. Those services may set their own cookies or collect information when you view or interact with them, under their own privacy policies. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies.

### How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law. Enquiries that do not lead to an ongoing relationship are generally deleted within 24 months. Newsletter details are kept until you unsubscribe.

### How we protect it

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information, including access controls and encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

### Your rights

You can ask to access the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or withdraw your consent. Send your request to our Privacy Officer; we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the [Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada](https://www.priv.gc.ca).
