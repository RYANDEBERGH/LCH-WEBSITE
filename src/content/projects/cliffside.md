The gateway to southwest Scarborough and home to the famous Scarborough Bluffs, Cliffside represents a unique mix of natural beauty and modern urban living. Cliffside is LCH Developments’ third project in Cliffside Village, across the street from Merge Condos and Merge Spaces, and continues our commitment to shaping a walkable and vibrant urban village.

Together with our partners at Cogir Real Estate, this 10-storey development will bring 209 purpose-built rental apartments and new retail space to the neighbourhood. Now under construction, Cliffside connects the energy of Kingston Road with the nearby lake, parks and waterfront trails. The building is designed to complement the village character of the area, with a planned four-storey mural on its east façade.
