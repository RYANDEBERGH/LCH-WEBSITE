Retreat Rentals at 3301 Kingston Road is uniquely situated just steps from one of Toronto’s most beautiful ravines. Gates Gully has captivated generations of nature enthusiasts on their journey from Kingston Road, down through the Scarborough Bluffs to the peaceful shores of Lake Ontario.

Developed in partnership with Fiera Real Estate, Retreat will bring 320 purpose-built rental apartments to this distinctive setting. Resting above the tree canopy on Windy Ridge Drive, the community is designed to offer inviting homes and a host of amenities that complement its serene surroundings. Retreat is an escape from the city that is just steps away from all that Scarborough has to offer.
