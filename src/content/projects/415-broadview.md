415 Broadview represents a unique opportunity to create a distinctive rental community overlooking Toronto’s Don River Valley. LCH Developments and deCartier Developments have been working with St. John’s Presbyterian Church on a plan that restores the historic church building, ensuring the congregation can continue to operate at the address it has called home since 1908.

The proposed development brings heritage restoration together with a modern residential addition incorporating mass-timber design. New purpose-built rental homes will add to the Riverdale neighbourhood while preserving the character and continued use of an important community landmark.
