For Akelius, LCH and Elevate prepared an in-depth, comprehensive development report on adding new purpose-built rental housing beside occupied buildings at 150 The Donway West. It brought together our development, design, construction and financial teams to test the approved scheme from every angle and set out a clear recommendation on how to proceed.

The report covered:

- Market and rental analysis, including comparable submarkets and lease-up assumptions
- A refined development proposal: simplified massing and structure, below-grade optimization, floor-to-floor efficiency and core reconfiguration
- Parking, servicing and interim access planning to keep existing residents' buildings operating during construction
- Approvals strategy and critical path for Site Plan Approval and minor variances, through to permits and construction readiness
- A refined construction budget, value-engineering review, cost benchmarks and consultant budget
- A full financial model with pro forma, rent roll, stabilized income and financing scenarios
- Conclusions and a staged path forward, with defined decision points
