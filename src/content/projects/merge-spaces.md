Merge Spaces is more than a project in our portfolio. LCH built it, and we own and operate it day to day. Our own office is here too, so we work alongside the members every day.

Opened in 2022 at 2229 Kingston Road in Cliffside Village, the two-storey space offers private offices, dedicated desks, coworking, meeting rooms, sound-proof phone booths, a games and lounge room, a café and a presentation and event space. Networking events, fireside chats and wellness sessions are part of the membership.

Running Merge Spaces gives us first-hand experience as an operator: what tenants and members need from a building, how amenity space actually gets used, and what it takes to make a place work long after construction ends. That experience feeds back into how we plan the commercial and amenity space in our own communities.
