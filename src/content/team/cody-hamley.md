Cody Hamley has over 6 years of experience working in accounting and finance at a variety of companies. He graduated from the University of Waterloo in 2019 receiving a Bachelor of Accounting and Financial Management degree. He started his career working in Public Accounting at Grant Thornton and obtained his CPA designation in 2022. Cody then transitioned into the real estate industry working at Brookfield Properties as a Property Accountant and Fitzrovia as a Senior Project Accountant before joining LCH Developments as the Accounting Manager. Cody has worked on both residential and commercial properties across the GTA.
