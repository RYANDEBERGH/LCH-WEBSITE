For the past few months, [LCH Developments](https://www.linkedin.com/company/lch-developments-to/) has donated our space at 2229 Kingston Road to the [Scarborough Food Security Initiative](https://www.linkedin.com/company/scarborough-food-security-initiative/), helping them serve over a thousand local residents during the pandemic.

In the future, we will be launching Merge Spaces; a co-working space to help Scarborough small businesses work and grow in a collaborative environment. We hope to see you when we open our doors in late 2021!
