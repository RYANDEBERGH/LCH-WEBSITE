A crane now stands tall at [Cliffside](https://urbantoronto.ca/database/projects/cliffside-condos.46722) in Scarborough’s Cliffside Village, following the start of shoring in August, 2026. Designed by [RAW Design](https://urbantoronto.ca/database/companies/raw-design.8045) for [LCH Developments](https://urbantoronto.ca/database/companies/lch-developments.17367), the 10-storey mixed-use rental building (formerly marketed as [Cliffside Condos](https://urbantoronto.ca/news/2024/05/lch-developments-launching-cliffside-condos-above-scarborough-bluffs.55918)), with retail planned at grade, is underway at the northeast corner of Kingston Road and Sharpe Street.
