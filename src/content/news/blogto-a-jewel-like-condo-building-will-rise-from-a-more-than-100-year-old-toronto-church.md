Declining church attendance, rising land value and an insatiable demand for new condos have created perfect conditions to bring [new uses](https://www.blogto.com/real-estate-toronto/2022/01/1183-dufferin-street-toronto/) to [some gorgeous old landmarks](https://www.blogto.com/real-estate-toronto/2021/07/historic-toronto-church-could-become-part-apartment-building-development/), a trend that’s been present throughout Toronto’s current condo boom.

But a growing sub-category in this trend is [maintaining the presence of congregations](https://www.blogto.com/real-estate-toronto/2019/07/toronto-church-condo-development/) in their proposals. The latest church property to go this route is [St. John’s Presbyterian Church](http://www.stjohnstoronto.com/) at [415 Broadview Avenue](https://goo.gl/maps/oj3oeroZvK8r7Qe96), a 1908-built landmark just east of the former Don Jail and current Bridgepoint Hospital.

A [development application was submitted](https://secure.toronto.ca/AIC/index.do?folderRsn=K2n6J9GzI354xMURtuibOg%3D%3D) with city planners in the final days of 2021. While supporting documents and renderings have yet to be made available to the public, [LCH Developments](https://lchdevelopments.com/), one of the developers behind the project, has shared details of the coming condominium building.
