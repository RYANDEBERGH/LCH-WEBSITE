Construction is approaching topping off at [Everston](https://urbantoronto.ca/database/projects/everston.47044) in Scarborough’s West Hill neighbourhood, where the 13-storey purpose-built rental development has now reached its full storey count at the northwest corner of Kingston Road and Manse Road. Designed by [Turner Fleischer](https://urbantoronto.ca/database/companies/turner-fleischer.8071) for [LCH Developments](https://urbantoronto.ca/database/companies/lch-developments.17367), the project in Toronto’s east end has progressed rapidly since construction began in July, 2025.
