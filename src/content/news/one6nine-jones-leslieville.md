Located in Toronto’s east end, Leslieville has emerged as one of the city’s hippest places to live. The area has attracted the attention of homebuyers and developers alike with its vibrant arts scene, trendy restaurants, boutique shops and convenient access to public transit.

Our development One6Nine Jones offers a collection of 12 modern luxury townhomes designed by Open Architects Inc. Livabl profiled the project in *One6Nine Jones offers a collection of modern and sophisticated townhomes in sought-after Leslieville*.
