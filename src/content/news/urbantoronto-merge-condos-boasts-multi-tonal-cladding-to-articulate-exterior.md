[Merge Condos](https://urbantoronto.ca/database/projects/merge-condos) have come a long way since we last checked in on the Kingston Road site in Scarborough’s Cliffside area about a year and a half ago. At the time, the [LCH Developments](https://urbantoronto.ca/database/companies/lch-developments) project was just having the first sections of its 2 ½-level underground garage built as crews simultaneously worked on forming its partial P3 and full P2 levels. Fast forward to today, the building is topped off at its final height of 12 storeys.
