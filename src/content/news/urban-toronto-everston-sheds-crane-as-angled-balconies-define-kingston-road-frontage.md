Sculpted, alternating balconies define the now mostly clad exterior of [Everston](https://urbantoronto.ca/database/projects/everston.47044), topped off and its crane removed since UrbanToronto’s last update in [May, 2026](https://urbantoronto.ca/news/2026/05/everston-nears-topping-scarboroughs-west-hill.61083). Designed by [Turner Fleischer](https://urbantoronto.ca/database/companies/turner-fleischer.8071) for [LCH Developments](https://urbantoronto.ca/database/companies/lch-developments.17367), the 13-storey development stands at the northwest corner of Kingston and Manse roads in Scarborough’s West Hill neighbourhood.
